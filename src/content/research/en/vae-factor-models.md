I make the factor network of the conditional autoencoder of Gu, Kelly, and Xiu (2021) probabilistic and show in closed form that variational training shrinks factor estimates toward the prior mean, with intensity set by the noise level and the KL weight. On U.S. stocks from 1957 to 2012, the conditional-prior model reaches an out-of-sample predictive R² of 1.08%–1.30%, versus 0.43%–0.93% for the baseline, and long-short Sharpe ratios of 3.19–3.45, with the gains concentrated in low-SNR environments.
